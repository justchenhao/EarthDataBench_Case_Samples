# FLUXNET2015 ET v0.4 source lineage

The paper uses FLUXNET2015 LE, ERA5-Land meteorology, and MODIS MYD13Q1.061 NDVI. This bounded three-site package exposes one checksum-pinned site/date-aligned public table, with every predictor column mapped to its paper source in `source_lineage.json`. Raw global ERA5-Land and MYD13Q1 granules are intentionally not redistributed; the case does not claim a full 64-site product.
